How to use The Playbook Caller

Browse

  • Pick Offense, Defense, Special (kicking units as circles, return and block units as triangles) or All in the left rail, narrow with family chips, the personnel-code menu, or search.
  • Click a formation: the eleven players slide to their new alignment. Hover or tap any player for role and responsibility.
  • Playbook (below the field: Browse all plays, or press P) draws common plays and coverages as arrows on the field. Run play (or R) moves the players and the ball along their arrows, Pause (or Space) freezes them, the speed buttons run it at half, normal or double speed, Reset returns them, and the PLAY chip clears the arrows.
  • Field position (above the field) spots the ball on your own 20 or 35, the 50, the opponent's 10 or the 3. The whole field is drawn, so scroll it or use Zoom to formation. Undo view and Redo view (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y when no editing mode is on) step back through position and zoom changes.
  • Draw (above the field, or press D) turns the field into a whiteboard: pick a notation (run, pass, block, motion, pitch, zone drop) and a colour, then drag to sketch, then Undo, Redo (also Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y while Draw is on) or Clear, which Undo can bring back. Start a stroke on a player and Run drawing (or Shift+R) animates him along it, with the ball going from the quarterback to the last player you drew for (or pick the receiver from the Ball to list in the Ball on group, or drag the ball onto any player; either way also sets the receiver of a custom play, and both are undoable with Ctrl+Z), and Save as play keeps it in the Custom section of the Playbook (stored in this browser and included in Export JSON), where Edit lets you rename it, change the notes and ball handling, or replace its paths with a new drawing (the Playbook group below the field lists the same plays by formation with Edit and Delete, and its Browse all plays button opens the full playbook), and Edit waypoints on field puts draggable handles on the arrows (drag to move a point, Shift+click to remove it, double-click to add one, Alt+click to switch an arrow between run, block and motion, Ctrl+click to delete a whole arrow, click a player who has no arrow to give him one, and Undo or Ctrl+Z to step back, Redo or Ctrl+Y to step forward again). Built-in plays have Edit a copy, which puts an editable copy in the Custom section. Strokes stay while you change formations and are included in exports.
  • Case studies (below the field, or press C) profile real offenses that ran these formations. A formation's History tab lists the studies that feature it.
  • Glossary (below the field, or press G) explains football terms by topic or A to Z, with search and links to related formations.
  • The right panel has collapsible Overview, Personnel, Strengths & Weaknesses, Situations, Counters and History sections, and a Legend dropdown under its header. The principal buttons below the field (Play, Editor, Ball on, Draw, Export) each show their controls with a tap and hide them with another; Playbook, Case studies, Glossary and How to use sit at the end of the same row. The bottom drawer holds the personnel-grouping and situational cheat sheets.

Overlay and compare

  • The Single / Overlay / Compare switch sits above the field, at the right. Overlay draws one offense and one defense on the same field. Clicking an offense replaces the offense; clicking a defense replaces the defense. Counter chips in the Counters tab jump straight to a matchup.
  • Compare has slots A and B. Click a slot (or a field) to make it active, then pick a formation. Same side = two fields side by side; offense vs defense = one overlaid field.
  • Defenses are drawn against a standard strong-right look and do not re-align to the offense you overlay.

Editor

  • Turn on Editor (below the field, next to Play), then drag players (snaps to half yards; hold Shift for tenths, or focus a player and use the arrow keys). Edit labels, roles and all metadata in the Edit tab; Undo and Redo there (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y when not typing) step through your changes. A live check flags illegal alignments.
  • Save as new formation stores a copy in this browser. Export JSON / Import JSON move formations between machines; import only adds custom formations and never overwrites built-ins. Reset to defaults removes all custom formations. Every stored change (saving, updating, deleting or importing formations; saving, copying, editing or deleting plays) can be reversed with Undo change / Redo in the Data menu and in the Playbook, or with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, which outside Draw and the editors always act on the last thing you changed: a play, the field view or the stored library.

Export

  • Download SVG saves the current diagram as a vector file. Copy as PNG puts a 2x image on the clipboard (or downloads it if the browser blocks clipboard access).

Keyboard

  • ← → previous / next formation in the filtered list
  • Alt+← Alt+→ back / forward through your selection history, which also records side, search and filter changes (also the Back and Forward buttons in the left rail)
